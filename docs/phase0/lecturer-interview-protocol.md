# GST Lecturer Interview Protocol

## Interview Overview
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Participants:** GST Course Coordinators and Lecturers
**Method:** Semi-structured interview + workflow observation
**Objective:** Deep understanding of current paper-based examination processes and pain points

---

## Interview Structure

### Part 1: Introduction & Context (5 minutes)
**Purpose:** Build rapport and set expectations

**Script:**
"Thank you for participating in our CBT system design process. We're here to learn from your expertise to ensure the new system addresses your real needs and challenges. This interview will help us understand your current workflow and identify opportunities for improvement. All responses will be confidential and used only for system design purposes."

### Part 2: Current Question Setting Process (15 minutes)

#### Question Creation Workflow
1. **How do you currently create examination questions?**
   - What tools or methods do you use?
   - Where do you source your content from?
   - How do you ensure question quality and relevance?

2. **Question Bank Management**
   - Do you maintain a question bank? If so, how?
   - How do you organize and categorize questions?
   - How do you track question usage and avoid repetition?

3. **Difficulty Balancing**
   - How do you ensure appropriate difficulty distribution?
   - What criteria do you use for question difficulty levels?
   - How do you verify difficulty before exams?

#### Quality Assurance Process
4. **Question Review Process**
   - Who reviews your questions before exams?
   - What are your review criteria?
   - How do you handle feedback and revisions?

5. **Version Control**
   - How do you track different versions of questions?
   - How do you manage last-minute changes?
   - What happens when questions need to be replaced?

### Part 3: Moderation & Approval Process (10 minutes)

#### Academic Quality Assurance
6. **Moderation Workflow**
   - Walk me through the moderation process step-by-step
   - Who is involved in moderation?
   - How long does moderation typically take?

7. **Compliance Checks**
   - What academic standards must questions meet?
   - How do you ensure curriculum alignment?
   - What documentation is required for Senate approval?

8. **Security During Moderation**
   - How do you maintain question confidentiality during moderation?
   - What are your concerns about question leakage?
   - What security measures are currently in place?

### Part 4: Printing & Logistics (10 minutes)

#### Physical Production
9. **Printing Process**
   - How do you handle question paper printing?
   - What are the security concerns during printing?
   - How do you manage printing logistics?

10. **Distribution Challenges**
    - How are exam papers distributed to venues?
    - What are the common issues during distribution?
    - How do you handle emergency printing needs?

#### Risk Management
11. **Contingency Planning**
    - What happens if papers are damaged or lost?
    - How do you handle printing errors?
    - What backup systems do you have?

### Part 5: Marking & Assessment (15 minutes)

#### Current Marking Workflow
12. **Marking Process**
    - Walk me through your marking process
    - How long does marking typically take per paper?
    - What tools do you use for marking?

13. **Quality Challenges**
    - What are the biggest challenges with handwritten answers?
    - How do you ensure marking consistency?
    - How do you handle illegible handwriting?

14. **Collation & Compilation**
    - How do you collate marks from multiple markers?
    - How do you handle marking disputes?
    - What verification processes do you use?

#### Feedback & Results
15. **Student Feedback**
    - How do you provide feedback to students?
    - What feedback do students typically request?
    - How long after exams do students receive results?

### Part 6: Senate Submission & Administration (5 minutes)

#### Administrative Requirements
16. **Reporting Requirements**
    - What reports do you need to prepare for Senate?
    - How do you compile grade statistics?
    - What documentation is required for audit purposes?

17. **Appeals & Revisions**
    - How do you handle grade appeals?
    - What is the process for grade revisions?
    - How often do appeals occur?

---

## Pain Point Discovery Questions

### Time & Efficiency
18. **What takes the most time in your current examination process?**
19. **Which part of the process would you most like to automate?**
20. **How much time could you save with an ideal system?**

### Quality & Accuracy
21. **Where do errors most frequently occur in your workflow?**
22. **What quality issues keep you awake at night during exam periods?**
23. **How do you currently ensure marking fairness and consistency?**

### Security & Integrity
24. **What are your biggest security concerns with paper-based exams?**
25. **How do you detect and handle examination misconduct?**
26. **What would make you feel more confident about exam security?**

### Student Experience
27. **What complaints do students most frequently have about exams?**
28. **How do you currently support students with special needs?**
29. **What would improve the student examination experience?**

---

## Vision & Requirements

### Ideal System Features
30. **If you could design the perfect examination system, what would it include?**
31. **What features would make your job significantly easier?**
32. **What would make you trust an automated grading system?**

### Adoption Concerns
33. **What concerns would you have about switching to CBT?**
34. **What support would you need during the transition?**
35. **What training would be helpful for you and your colleagues?**

## Observation Protocol

### Workflow Observation Session
**Duration:** 30-45 minutes following interview
**Focus:** Real-time observation of actual examination preparation activities

#### Areas to Observe:
1. **Question Bank Access**
   - How questions are stored and retrieved
   - Search and filter methods used
   - Organization and categorization systems

2. **Question Editing Process**
   - Tools and software used
   - Version control methods
   - Quality checks performed

3. **Collaboration Methods**
   - How feedback is shared
   - Revision tracking
   - Approval workflows

4. **Time Measurements**
   - Time spent per question
   - Review cycle duration
   - Administrative overhead

---

## Documentation Requirements

### Interview Notes Structure
1. **Participant Information**
   - Role and experience level
   - Types of courses taught
   - Previous CBT experience

2. **Current Process Mapping**
   - Step-by-step workflow documentation
   - Time measurements for each step
   - Tools and resources used

3. **Pain Point Classification**
   - Time-related issues
   - Quality concerns
   - Security challenges
   - User experience problems

4. **Requirements Identification**
   - Must-have features
   - Nice-to-have features
   - Non-functional requirements
   - Integration needs

---

## Follow-up Actions

### Post-Interview Process
1. **Send thank-you note within 24 hours**
2. **Share summary of key points for validation**
3. **Invite to requirements review session**
4. **Include in user testing phase**

### Validation Requirements
1. **Review captured requirements with participants**
2. **Clarify any ambiguous points**
3. **Prioritize requirements by importance**
4. **Identify conflicting requirements between lecturers**

---

## Interview Logistics

### Scheduling
- **Best Times:** Between lectures, office hours
- **Location:** Private office or meeting room
- **Recording:** Audio recording with permission
- **Materials:** Interview guide, consent form, note-taking tools

### Preparation Checklist
- [ ] Confirm interview time and location
- [ ] Prepare recording device
- [ ] Print interview guides
- [ ] Obtain consent forms
- [ ] Prepare note-taking templates
- [ ] Test audio recording equipment

### Data Privacy
- **Consent:** Written consent for recording and data use
- **Anonymization:** Remove personal identifiers in reports
- **Storage:** Secure storage of interview recordings
- **Access:** Limited to core project team only
