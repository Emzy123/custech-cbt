# Examination Officer Process Mapping Guide

## Process Mapping Overview
**Objective:** Document end-to-end examination logistics workflow
**Participants:** Examination Officers from each faculty
**Method:** Structured walkthrough + time measurement + pain point identification
**Duration:** 90 minutes per session

---

## Current Process Analysis Framework

### 1. Venue Allocation Process

#### Pre-Exam Planning
**Key Questions:**
1. **How do you currently allocate venues for examinations?**
   - What factors influence venue selection?
   - How do you determine venue capacity?
   - What system or tools do you use for allocation?

2. **Capacity Management**
   - How do you calculate required venue capacity?
   - What buffer capacity do you maintain?
   - How do you handle over-subscribed venues?

3. **Special Accommodations**
   - How do you allocate venues for students with special needs?
   - What accessibility requirements do you consider?
   - How do you coordinate with disability support services?

**Time Metrics to Capture:**
- Time to allocate venues for a typical exam session
- Time to resolve venue conflicts
- Time to arrange special accommodations

**Pain Points to Identify:**
- Venue availability constraints
- Capacity calculation challenges
- Communication breakdowns
- Last-minute changes

### 2. Invigilator Management

#### Staffing Process
**Key Questions:**
1. **Invigilator Recruitment**
   - How do you recruit and train invigilators?
   - What qualifications do you require?
   - How do you ensure adequate coverage?

2. **Scheduling Process**
   - How do you create invigilator schedules?
   - What is the optimal invigilator-to-student ratio?
   - How do you handle invigilator availability conflicts?

3. **Training & Briefing**
   - What training do invigilators receive?
   - How do you brief them for specific exams?
   - What procedures do they need to know?

**Time Metrics to Capture:**
- Time to recruit and train invigilators
- Time to create exam schedules
- Time for pre-exam briefings

**Pain Points to Identify:**
- Staffing shortages
- Scheduling conflicts
- Training effectiveness
- Communication issues

### 3. Attendance Tracking Process

#### Student Management
**Key Questions:**
1. **Attendance Methods**
   - How do you currently track student attendance?
   - What tools or systems do you use?
   - How do you handle late arrivals?

2. **Identity Verification**
   - How do you verify student identities?
   - What documentation do you require?
   - How do you handle identity disputes?

3. **Absence Management**
   - How do you document student absences?
   - What is the process for excused absences?
   - How do you handle medical emergencies?

**Time Metrics to Capture:**
- Time to mark attendance per 100 students
- Time to verify student identities
- Time to process attendance records

**Pain Points to Identify:**
- Manual data entry errors
- Identity verification challenges
- Late arrival disruptions
- Record keeping issues

### 4. Material Distribution Process

#### Logistics Management
**Key Questions:**
1. **Preparation Phase**
   - How do you prepare exam materials?
   - What quality checks do you perform?
   - How do you package materials for distribution?

2. **Distribution Process**
   - How do you distribute materials to venues?
   - What security measures do you implement?
   - How do you track material movement?

3. **Collection Process**
   - How do you collect completed exams?
   - What verification processes do you use?
   - How do you handle missing materials?

**Time Metrics to Capture:**
- Time to prepare materials per venue
- Time to distribute materials to all venues
- Time to collect and verify completed exams

**Pain Points to Identify:**
- Material security concerns
- Distribution delays
- Tracking accuracy
- Damage or loss issues

### 5. Emergency Management

#### Incident Response
**Key Questions:**
1. **Common Emergencies**
   - What emergencies typically occur during exams?
   - How do you categorize emergency severity?
   - What protocols do you follow?

2. **Response Procedures**
   - How do you coordinate emergency responses?
   - What communication channels do you use?
   - How do you document incidents?

3. **Contingency Planning**
   - What backup systems do you have?
   - How do you handle venue evacuations?
   - What are your continuity procedures?

**Time Metrics to Capture:**
- Response time for different emergency types
- Time to resolve common issues
- Time to implement contingency plans

**Pain Points to Identify:**
- Inadequate emergency protocols
- Communication failures
- Resource shortages
- Documentation gaps

## Process Mapping Methodology

### Workflow Documentation
For each process area, document:

1. **Process Steps**
   - Sequential order of activities
   - Decision points and branches
   - Handoffs between roles

2. **Roles & Responsibilities**
   - Who performs each step
   - What authority they have
   - Who they report to

3. **Tools & Systems**
   - What software or tools are used
   - How data flows between systems
   - What manual processes exist

4. **Time Measurements**
   - Duration of each step
   - Wait times between steps
   - Total process cycle time

5. **Quality Controls**
   - What checks are performed
   - How errors are detected
   - What correction processes exist

### Pain Point Classification
Categorize identified issues as:

1. **Time-Related Issues**
   - Process bottlenecks
   - Excessive wait times
   - Inefficient workflows

2. **Resource Constraints**
   - Staffing shortages
   - Venue limitations
   - Equipment failures

3. **Communication Breakdowns**
   - Information gaps
   - Misaligned expectations
   - Coordination failures

4. **Quality Concerns**
   - Data accuracy issues
   - Process inconsistencies
   - Error rates

5. **Security Risks**
   - Material security
   - Data protection
   - Access control

## CBT System Requirements Discovery

### Digital Transformation Opportunities
For each current process, identify:

1. **Automation Potential**
   - What manual steps can be automated?
   - What decisions can be systemized?
   - What data can be captured automatically?

2. **Integration Needs**
   - What systems need to be connected?
   - What data flows are required?
   - What APIs are needed?

3. **User Interface Requirements**
   - What screens are needed for each role?
   - What information must be displayed?
   - What actions must be supported?

4. **Reporting Requirements**
   - What reports are needed?
   - What metrics must be tracked?
   - What alerts are required?

### Security & Compliance
1. **Access Control**
   - Who needs access to what functions?
   - What authentication is required?
   - What audit trails are needed?

2. **Data Protection**
   - What sensitive data is handled?
   - How must it be protected?
   - What retention policies apply?

3. **Compliance Requirements**
   - What regulations must be followed?
   - What standards must be met?
   - What documentation is required?

## Observation Protocol

### On-Site Observation
**Duration:** 2-3 hours during actual exam period
**Focus:** Real-time process execution

#### Areas to Observe:
1. **Student Check-in Process**
   - Queue management
   - Identity verification
   - Attendance recording

2. **Exam Distribution**
   - Material handling
   - Security procedures
   - Timing coordination

3. **Invigilation**
   - Monitoring methods
   - Issue handling
   - Communication patterns

4. **Exam Collection**
   - Verification procedures
   - Packaging methods
   - Transfer processes

#### Data Collection:
- Time measurements for each step
- Error rates and correction times
- Staff utilization patterns
- Student flow and bottlenecks

## Follow-up Process

### Validation Sessions
1. **Review captured processes with participants**
2. **Clarify ambiguous steps or decisions**
3. **Verify time measurements and data**
4. **Prioritize improvement opportunities**

### Requirements Workshop
1. **Present process analysis findings**
2. **Identify digital transformation opportunities**
3. **Define system requirements collaboratively**
4. **Prioritize requirements by impact and effort**

### Change Management Planning
1. **Assess change impact on current roles**
2. **Identify training and support needs**
3. **Plan communication strategy**
4. **Define transition approach**
